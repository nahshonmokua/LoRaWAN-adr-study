# Figure coverage and agreement check

The fresh rerun generated five PNG files representing six paper figures: 4, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15. SHA256 hashes of all five PNGs match the pre-existing project outputs exactly. This establishes repeatability of those project figures, not agreement with the paper.

Figures 1–3 and 5–7 (system/setup diagrams, photographs, workflow and architecture illustrations) were not recreated. Figures 8–10 (ANN/SVR/RF hyperparameter and cross-validation plots) were not regenerated by the final pipeline.

All five generated PNGs were visually inspected against their corresponding paper figures. The supplied PDF pages 4 and 14 were rendered for Figures 4 and 14–15; existing high-resolution paper extracts were inspected for Figures 11–13.

| Figure | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 4 | Mean path-loss and model positions are broadly similar; the observation spread differs visibly and is displayed as ranges rather than the paper’s point clouds. No complete digitized point comparison is available. |
| 11 | Broadly close; RMSE 1.72 percentage points, maximum discrepancy 7.02 points. Excludes acknowledged unreadable Friis points at LM 3–8. |
| 12 | Material mismatch: RMSE 11.44 percentage points, maximum discrepancy 30.88 points. |
| 13 | Material mismatch: RMSE 14.63 percentage points, maximum discrepancy 39.44 points. Winner differs: SVR in the reproduction, ANN in the paper. |
| 14–15 | Not exact reproduction: tail patterns differ visibly; Student-t QQ correlation squared is 0.9869 versus the paper’s 0.996. The reproduction uses synthetically perturbed observations. |

The numerical comparison uses outputs/final/figs_vs_paper_digitized.csv. Digitization uncertainty is approximately ±2 percentage points; differences in Figures 12–13 substantially exceed that uncertainty. At the labeled 95% PDR operating point, Figure 13 gives RF energy improvement of 36.41%, versus approximately 11.04% digitized from the paper. At 99%, the paper text reports ANN/SVR/RF 43.5/40.6/38.7%, versus 45.5/49.2/46.8% here.
